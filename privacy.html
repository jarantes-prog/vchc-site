vchc privacy policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

What vchc accesses

Only the Google account of the channel owner who runs it, with that owner's permission:

  • YouTube: upload videos to the owner's channel, and read the owner's own videos and YouTube Analytics.
  • Gmail: send email from the owner's account. vchc uses this only to send reports and alerts to the owner.

vchc also reads public YouTube information, such as video titles and view counts, to find clips.

Where the data goes

Everything stays on the owner's own computer. The Google sign-in is stored in the macOS Keychain. vchc has no server. It does not collect personal data about anyone else, and it does not sell, share or transfer any data.

vchc's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

YouTube

vchc uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and the Google Privacy Policy applies.

Removing access

You can remove vchc's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
